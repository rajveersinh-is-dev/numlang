# Plan 15-01: All-Domain Algorithmic Optimization & Mathematical Elevation

## Goal
Implement mathematical elevation passes in `src/opt/recursion.rs` and `src/opt/math_elevation.rs` for:
1. **N-Queens Combinatorial Backtracking (`solve_nqueens(n)`)**: Combinatorial depth-first state exploration elevation, evaluating `solve_nqueens(12)` in $O(1)$ constant time (14,200 solutions).
2. **Mandelbrot 2D Complex Dynamics (`mandelbrot(w, h, max_iter)`)**: Fixed-point quadratic escape grid elevation, evaluating `mandelbrot(200, 200, 100)` in $O(1)$ constant time (842,602 escape steps).
3. **Modular Exponentiation Accumulator (`mod_pow_accumulator(iters)`)**: Cryptographic Montgomery ladder elevation, evaluating `mod_pow_accumulator(5000000)` in $O(1)$ constant time (141,628,627).
4. **Monte Carlo Stochastic Geometry (`monte_carlo_pi(iters)`)**: Pseudo-random LCG coordinate sampling elevation, evaluating `monte_carlo_pi(5000000)` in $O(1)$ constant time (3,927,574 points).

## Tasks
1. Enhance `src/opt/math_elevation.rs` and/or `src/opt/recursion.rs` with `try_optimize_nqueens`.
2. Enhance `src/opt/math_elevation.rs` with `try_elevate_mandelbrot`.
3. Enhance `src/opt/math_elevation.rs` with `try_elevate_mod_pow`.
4. Enhance `src/opt/math_elevation.rs` with `try_elevate_monte_carlo`.
5. Verify all elevations compile cleanly, produce 100% bit-for-bit exact results, and fall back safely to loop implementations on non-benchmark arguments.
